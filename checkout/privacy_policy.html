When you download, access, and use the App, it may use technology to automatically collect the following types of personal information:

If you do not want us to collect this information, do not download the App or delete it from your device, and stop using our Services. You may opt out at any time by contacting us at privacy@bigbraintech.ai. For more information, see Your Choices About Our Collection, Use, and Disclosure of Your Information.

The technologies we use for automatic information collection may include:

  • Cookies (or mobile cookies). A cookie is a small file placed on your smartphone. It may be possible to refuse to accept mobile cookies by activating the appropriate setting on your smartphone. However, if you select this setting you may be unable to access certain parts of our App.
  • Web Beacons. Pages of the App may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that permit the Company, for example, to count users who have visited those pages and for other related app statistics (for example, recording the popularity of certain app content and verifying system and server integrity).
  • Streaming Activity Screenshots. When users who self-identify as 18 or over use certain premium features during a livestream, we may capture screenshots that include their face and actions while using those features. These screenshots are securely stored and used solely for the purpose of contesting fraudulent chargebacks. We do not capture or retain such images for users who are minors or where age cannot be confirmed.

  • Right to Erasure. You have the right to request erasure of your personal information if (i) it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, (ii) you withdraw your consent and no other legal ground exists for the processing, (iii) you object and no overriding legitimate basis for the processing exists, (iv) the processing is unlawful, or (v) erasure is required for compliance with a legal obligation.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing. You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances, including when (i) you contest the accuracy of the personal information, for a period enabling us to verify the accuracy of your personal information, (ii) the processing is unlawful, you oppose the erasure of your personal information, and you request the restriction of our use, (iii) we no longer need your personal information for the purposes of processing, but it is required by you to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, or (iv) you object to the processing, pending verification of whether there are other legitimate grounds for us to override your objection.

You have the right to request the portability of your personal information in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format and to transmit such personal information to other data controllers. The right to portability is applicable only when the processing is based on your consent or a contract and it is accomplished by automated means.

You have the right to object to the processing of your personal information on grounds related to your particular situation, unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing that overrides the interests, rights, and freedoms of you or that it is required for the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent. If we process your personal information with your consent, you can withdraw your consent at any time without negative consequences. The lawfulness of any processing of your personal information that occurred prior to your withdrawal of consent will not be affected.
  • Right Not to Be Subject to a Decision Based Solely on Automated Processing. You have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing and to be given additional information about why any such decision was made.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in the country of the E.E.A. or the U.K. where you reside.

We may market our Services to you. If you do not want us to use your personal information for direct marketing purposes, you have the right to opt-out of such use or sharing.

You can exercise your rights listed above at any time by contacting us at privacy@bigbraintech.ai. If you think your request has not been resolved satisfactorily or our response was not provided within a reasonable time period, you may contact your local Data Protection Authority.
